What the stabilizers did

A stabilizer evens out one kind of difference that says nothing about the code, on both sides, before they are compared again. Its tier says how much it may disturb, from least to most: structural reorders or reframes an archive, or leaves out a signature or checksum computed over its files; metadata rewrites fields that are not a file's contents, such as timestamps and permissions; content rewrites bytes inside a file; lossy leaves content out. The riskier its tier, the more a reader should check what it did.
