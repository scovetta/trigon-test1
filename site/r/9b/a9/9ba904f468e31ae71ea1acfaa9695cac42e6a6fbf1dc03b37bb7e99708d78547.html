What the stabilizers did

A stabilizer evens out one kind of difference that says nothing about the code, on both sides, before they are compared again. Its tier says how much it may disturb, from least to most: structural reorders or reframes an archive, or leaves out a signature or checksum computed over its files; metadata rewrites fields that are not a file's contents, such as timestamps and permissions; content rewrites bytes inside a file; lossy leaves content out. The riskier its tier, the more a reader should check what it did.

stabilizerwhat it evens outriskentries rewritten (both sides)
gzip-meta-v2clears the gzip header: when it was compressed, on which system, and the file namemetadata2
tar-entry-order-v2puts the files of a tar archive in name orderstructural20
tar-modesets every file's permissions in a tar archive to one valuemetadata20
tar-timesets every file's timestamps in a tar archive to one valuemetadata20

Entries are counted on both sides together, whether or not a value already matched: a pass that rewrote the timestamps of 10 files on each side rewrote 20.
